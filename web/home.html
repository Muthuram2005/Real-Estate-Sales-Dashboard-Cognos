Analytics Dashboard

The Real Estate Sales Dashboard is an interactive web-based analytics application designed to transform large volumes of Airbnb property data into clear and meaningful visual insights. Built using IBM Cognos Analytics, the platform enables users to explore market behaviour through dynamic charts, real-time metrics, and comparative visualizations. It simplifies complex datasets by presenting trends related to pricing, property type, host activity, location-based performance, and customer engagement in an intuitive single-view interface. By converting raw listings into actionable business intelligence, the website helps investors, hosts, and analysts make informed decisions, identify profitable opportunities, and understand regional market patterns with ease and accuracy.
